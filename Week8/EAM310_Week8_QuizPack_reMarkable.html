ANSWER KEY
Part A — Active Recall
#Answer
1Hearing and Smelling — the 2nd of the Four Diagnostic Methods (望聞問切).
2Lung=production, Kidney=volume, Spleen & Stomach=strength.
3Loud=Full/Heat. Weak=Empty/Cold.
4Blood stagnation=chronic, gradual intensity. Yin deficiency=chronic, gradual, worse in the evening.
5Excess/Heat: loud sound, rough/distressed breath, acute onset. Empty/Cold: low sound, faint/slow breath, slow onset.
6Rebellious Stomach Qi.
7Strong/foul/rotten = Heat or Excess. Absent or fishy = Cold or Deficient.
8Shout=Wood, Laugh=Fire, Sing=Earth, Weep=Metal, Groan=Water.
9Xū=Liver, Hē=Heart, Hū=Spleen, Sì=Lung, Chuī=Kidney, Xī=Triple Burner.
10Body odor=pattern + constitutional type. Odor of secretions=nature of the pattern only.
11Rancid, Scorched, Fragrant, Rotten, Putrid.
12It's labeled with xiào 笑 (same hanzi as Laughing) — classical scorched is jiāo 焦.
13Strong/rotten=Heat in Lungs, usually Phlegm-Heat or Toxic Heat. Fishy=Lung-Heat.
14Heart fire pouring downward into the Bladder.
15The Shouting voice sound (Wood) AND the Spleen Healing Sound — same hanzi, different tables.
