ANSWER KEY
#AnsWhy
1DThe slides assign only three organs to the voice — Lung (production), Kidney (volume), Spleen/Stomach (strength). Liver is not one of the three voice-governing organs on these slides.
2DReluctance to talk indicates a Cold pattern or Lung-Qi deficiency, not Full/Heat.
3DLung/Kidney Yin deficiency hoarseness is described as worse in the EVENING, not the morning.
4DA surplus of external contraction of evil qi (or phlegm-heat) is the Excess/Heat mechanism, not Empty/Cold. Empty/Cold is from internal damage causing insufficient Zheng Qi and Kidney/Lung Qi vacuity.
5DShortness of Breath is attributed to Lung qi deficiency or Water Retention in the Chest, not excess Heart fire.
6DA quiet, low hiccup sound indicates an Empty pattern (often ST-Qi or ST-Yin deficiency), not Full/Excess.
7DSighing generally indicates Qi stagnation of the Liver or Lungs (from repressed anger/frustration or worry/sadness), or Spleen/Heart deficiency from sadness/grief/pensiveness — not Kidney Yin deficiency.
8CEarth pairs with Singing, not Groaning. Groaning pairs with Water.
9CChuī 吹 targets the Kidney, not the Spleen. The Spleen Healing Sound is Hū 呼.
10DA strong smell indicates Heat or Excess, the opposite of what this option states.
11CThe five named odors on these slides are Rancid, Scorched, Fragrant, Rotten, and Putrid. "Sour" is not one of the five named odor slides (though it appears separately as a Breath odor finding).
12DUnlike the Five Element Sounds slides, the Five Odor slides do NOT explicitly pair each odor with an element.
13DFishy breath odor indicates Phlegm-Heat in the Lung. Heart fire pouring into the Bladder is the pattern for a heavy urea smell in urine, not breath.
14CSputum without smell indicates Cold, not Heat.
15DSlight or odorless flatulence aggravated by emotional stress indicates Liver qi stagnation. Toxic Heat in the Large Intestines is indicated by flatulence that smells rancid and rotten like rotten eggs.
16DOdorless urinary incontinence indicates exhaustion of Kidney fire, not Liver Qi stagnation.
17DRed, swollen eyes with turbid tears and foul, fishy odor (or ulcerations) indicates Damp-Heat in the LIVER channel, not the Spleen channel.
18DNo pinyin/hanzi is given for the Weeping voice sound on these slides. Hū 呼 is the Shouting voice sound and the Spleen Healing Sound.
