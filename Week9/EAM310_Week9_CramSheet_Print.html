2 · Sleep

Sleep depends on Blood & Yin, esp. Heart & Liver (Spleen has influence too). Mind/Shen + Hun must be anchored by HT/LV Blood.

FindingPattern
Can't fall/stay asleepBlood def. not anchoring Shen/Hun (Empty)
Restless, hot, agitated, excess dreamingPathogens agitating Shen/Hun out of Blood (Full)
Difficulty falling asleepBlood deficiency (usually HT, can be LV/SP)
Waking many times at nightYin def. with Empty Heat (HT/LV/KI)
Waking early, can't return to sleepGall Bladder deficiency
Insomnia + anxiety, dream-disturbed, Qi/Blood signsHT and SP Blood deficiency
Insomnia + difficulty falling asleep, dizzy, blurred visionLiver-Blood deficiency
Insomnia, waking freq., excess dreams, night sweat, palpitationsHT and KI-Yin def. with Empty-Heat
Dream-disturbed sleep (isolated)Liver-Fire or Heart-Fire
Restless sleep, excess dreamsRetention of food
Quick Recall: FALLING asleep=Heart Blood. DREAMING=Liver. WAKING repeatedly=Yin deficiency/Empty Heat.
Heads Up — Dream-symbolism material (Nèijīng passages, Po/Hun/Shen dream categories) is explicitly "not tested" per Schrier's own slide. Skip drilling it.

TrapCorrect Discriminator

All 5 odors map cleanly to 5 ElementsSlides don't label element per odor; don't assume the classical pairing without confirming

Heads Up — Quiz Move: For every Inquiry/hearing/smelling finding: quality (loud/foul vs. weak/faint, timing, location) → Full/Excess/Heat vs. Empty/Deficiency/Cold → organ → one confirming detail.
