How to use this pack: Negative-format questions ("All of the following EXCEPT...") are where students lose points even when they know the material — one wrong-in-context option hides among three correct ones. ANSWER KEY
#AnsWhy
1DPain in the limbs indicates Qi or Blood Stagnation, not Liver-Yang rising.
2DTremors can be EITHER excess or deficiency-type Wind, per the slides — not excess-type only.
3DCold hands with palpitations and dizziness indicates Heart-Blood deficiency, not Kidney-Yang deficiency.
4DProf. Schrier's own slide states dream-symbolism material "Will Not Be Tested On."
5DRestless sleep with excessive dreams indicates retention of food, not Kidney-Yang deficiency.
6DOily, pearl-like sweat on the forehead indicates Collapse of Yang and signals imminent death — not a good prognosis, and not simply Damp-Heat.
7DA low-pitched noise like rushing water indicates Kidney deficiency, not Liver-Fire (which is a high-pitched presentation).
8BFever intensity depends on the Zheng Qi vs. pathogen struggle, explicitly NOT on whether the pathogen is Wind-Cold or Wind-Heat.
9DHeat in the afternoon/evening indicates Empty-Heat/Yin deficiency, not exclusively Damp-Heat.
10DEvery other organ is AFFECTED by emotion without being conscious of it — the statement inverts this relationship.
11DManic behavior, mental confusion, and a swollen tongue indicates Phlegm-Fire harassing the Mind, not Heart-Blood deficiency (which is an Empty pattern, not a Full anxiety presentation).
12DExcessive sexual desire mechanisms (Liver/Heart-Fire, Empty-Heat from Kidney-Yin deficiency) apply to both sexes; Maciocia's text just happens to list it only under Women.
13CPain BEFORE the period indicates stagnation of Qi or Blood, not Blood deficiency (which is the pattern for pain AFTER the period).
14DThe 3-month cutoff is the key differentiator: before 3 months leans deficiency; after 3 months leans stasis/sinking.
15DChronic earache in children is specifically Damp-Heat in the Gall Bladder channel — a channel-pathway-based answer, not a generic Kidney deficiency.
16DThe slides assign only three organs to the voice — Lung (production), Kidney (volume), Spleen/Stomach (strength). Liver is not one of the three.
17DLung/Kidney Yin deficiency hoarseness is described as worse in the EVENING, not the morning.
18DA surplus of external contraction of evil qi (or phlegm-heat) is the Excess/Heat mechanism, not Empty/Cold.
19DShortness of Breath is attributed to Lung qi deficiency or Water Retention in the Chest, not excess Heart fire.
20DA quiet, low hiccup sound indicates an Empty pattern, not Full/Excess.
21CEarth pairs with Singing, not Groaning. Groaning pairs with Water.
22CChuī 吹 targets the Kidney, not the Spleen. The Spleen Healing Sound is Hū 呼.
23DA strong smell indicates Heat or Excess, the opposite of what this option states.
24DUnlike the Five Element Sounds slides, the Five Odor slides do NOT explicitly pair each odor with an element.
25DOdorless urinary incontinence indicates exhaustion of Kidney fire, not Liver Qi stagnation.
