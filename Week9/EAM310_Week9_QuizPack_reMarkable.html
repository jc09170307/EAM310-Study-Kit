ANSWER KEY
Part A — Active Recall
#Answer
1Tingling = Blood deficiency; numbness (esp. unilateral) = Wind or Wind-Phlegm.
2Cold fingertips especially = Liver-Qi stagnation (Liver Qi can't send Qi/Blood to the extremities, tips especially, while the torso stays warm).
3Heart and Liver (Spleen also has influence).
4Falling asleep = Heart-Blood deficiency (usually). Excessive dreaming = Liver. Waking repeatedly = Yin deficiency with Empty Heat.
5Prof. Schrier's own slide states "Bonus Material on Sleep Will Not Be Tested On."
6Yin deficiency; called "five-palm heat."
7Daytime = Yang deficiency. Night = Yin deficiency.
8Sudden = Full (Liver-Fire, Liver-Yang rising, or Liver-Wind). Gradual = Empty (Kidney deficiency).
9The struggle between Zheng Qi (Upright Qi) and the External Pathogenic Factor — not whether the pathogen is Wind-Cold or Wind-Heat.
10Aversion to cold = subjective (patient feels cold). Fever = objective (body feels hot on palpation).
11The Heart alone houses the Shen/consciousness and "knows" what an emotion is; every other organ is affected by emotion without being conscious of it.
12Heart-Blood deficiency; Heart-Yin deficiency (worse in evening, night sweating); Heart and Gall Bladder Qi deficiency (with timidity).
13Heart-Blood stasis — severe anxiety, palpitations, stabbing/pricking chest pain radiating to the inner left arm or shoulder, purple tongue.
14Younger men's premature ejaculation is often more about settling the Heart / performance anxiety; older men's is more truly Kidney-rooted.
15Pain BEFORE the period = stagnation of Qi or Blood. Pain AFTER the period = Blood deficiency.
16Before 3 months = Blood or Essence deficiency. After 3 months = Liver-Blood stasis, or sinking of Spleen-Qi.
17The Gall Bladder channel has a documented deep pathway to the ear; know deep pathways, not just the superficial map.
18Hearing and Smelling — the 2nd of the Four Diagnostic Methods (望聞問切).
19Lung=production, Kidney=volume, Spleen & Stomach=strength.
20Rebellious Stomach Qi.
21Shout=Wood, Laugh=Fire, Sing=Earth, Weep=Metal, Groan=Water.
22Xū=Liver, Hē=Heart, Hū=Spleen, Sì=Lung, Chuī=Kidney, Xī=Triple Burner.
23Strong/foul/rotten = Heat or Excess. Absent or fishy = Cold or Deficient.
24It's labeled with xiào 笑 (same hanzi as Laughing) — classical scorched is jiāo 焦.
25The Shouting voice sound (Wood) AND the Spleen Healing Sound — same hanzi, different tables.
