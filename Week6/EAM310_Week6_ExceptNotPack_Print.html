How to use this pack: Negative-format questions ("All of the following EXCEPT...") are where students lose points even when they know the material — one wrong-in-context option hides among three correct ones. ANSWER KEY
#AnsWhy
1DSun Simiao is not one of the three cited sources for Zang-Xiang; this is a distractor pattern (constitution ≠ Zang-Xiang).
2DConstitution assessment is a separate topic; the four methods are Inspection, Auscultation & Olfaction, Inquiry, Palpation.
3D"Dull, then suddenly bright" describes False Shen, not Having Shen.
4CNight sweats accompany the deficiency-type (malar flush) presentation specifically, not red color in general.
5DDeep yellow + bloodshot indicates toxic heat, not kidney yang insufficiency (that's a Dark Grey/Black finding).
6DThere is no "Fire Wheel." The pupil is the Water Wheel, mapped to Kidney, not Heart.
7DWeak voice and slight cough with sunken eyes indicate Lung deficiency, not Liver Yang rising.
8CWhite rings around the iris indicate turbid phlegm, not heat.
9DThe nose's five-phase correspondence is Lung, not Kidney.
10CThick yellow discharge indicates a Heat pattern.
11DSan Jiao is involved in fluid metabolism generally but is NOT one of the three yin organs tested for edema starting-location logic.
12CHigh-pitch tinnitus indicates Liver, not Kidney. Low-pitch indicates Kidney.
13DRed ear color indicates excess heat (possibly damp-heat), not deficiency.
14DDeviated/missing tooth growth indicates insufficient Yangming Qi, not Liver Blood deficiency.
15DA blue line between teeth and gums indicates mercury poisoning, not Kidney Yin deficiency.
16DPale lips indicate blood deficiency, a cold/deficiency pattern, not heat.
17DPale red throat indicates empty (deficiency) heat, not excess heat.
18CPremature graying indicates depletion of Kidney essence, the opposite of sufficiency.
19CSlow return after blanching indicates EXHAUSTION of Qi and Blood, not sufficiency — immediate return indicates sufficiency.
20DA lustrous, harmonious complexion is the NORMAL finding, not abnormal.
21D"Wheal" was not one of the four lesion types covered (Papule, Macule, Blister, Nodule).
22DInsufficiency of Qi and Blood describes the Yin-type carbuncle (Ju), not the Yang-type (Yong).
23DStrong odor indicates Heat, not cold — no odor/faint odor indicates cold.
24DThis technique is infant-only (age <3) and does not show reliably on adults.
25DVein extending beyond the Life Gate to the fingertip indicates life-threatening illness, not mild/resolving.
26DGreen or dark blue indicates Wind, not cold.
27DThe Su Wen 32 face map is a distinct framework from the Five Wheels of the Eye — one maps face regions, the other maps eye sub-structures.
28DFacial Sheen is associated with Qi and Yang; Facial Color (not Sheen) is associated with Blood and Yin.
29D"Suddenly responsive" after decline describes False Shen, not Loss of Shen.
30CBái hé bìng (Lily Disease) presents as chronic depression and sleepwalking — raving/obscenities/wandering naked describes Kuáng (Yang Madness).
31DLarge, overweight thighs indicate Spleen not transforming/transporting, not Kidney Yin deficiency.
32CSmall, restless fidgeting (e.g., leg bouncing) indicates Empty (deficiency) heat, not Excess Yang.
33CLong ears with long earlobes indicate a STRONG constitution, not weak.
34DDark complexion, big head, and wrinkles describe the Water type, not Metal (Metal = pale complexion, square face).
