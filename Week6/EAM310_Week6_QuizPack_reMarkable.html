Part A — Active Recall
#Answer
1Internal organ states manifest on the body surface — what's inside shows on the outside.
2Inspection (eyes: vitality/color/appearance/secretions), Auscultation & Olfaction (ears+nose), Inquiry (verbal questioning), Palpation (touch, incl. pulse).
3Vitality/spirit; reflected in the eyes and complexion, plus tongue and pulse.
4Red=heat, Pale/White=cold/blood-deficiency, Yellow=deficiency/damp, Blue-Green=cold/pain/stagnation, Dark Grey/Black=kidney-deficiency/blood-stasis.
5Clear/white = deficiency and cold. Turbid/yellow = excess and heat.
6Blood Wheel=canthus=Heart; Qi Wheel=sclera=Lung; Wind Wheel=iris=Liver; Water Wheel=pupil=Kidney; Flesh Wheel=eyelids=Spleen/Stomach.
7Pericardium/San Jiao — NOT Kidney directly (common simplified-teaching error).
8Five-phase correspondence (Lung), and facial maps — both Su Wen center-of-face and Ling Shu bridge-of-nose (Spleen).
9Lung (upper body/head), Kidney (lower body/feet), Spleen (four limbs/torso).
10Kidney; long+full earlobes = strong constitution, small/short = poor constitution.
11High-pitch = Liver (like cicadas). Low-pitch = Kidney (like rushing water).
12Kidney (teeth = surplus of bone); white/moist/bright = strong Kidney Qi, dry/no sheen = Kidney Yin decline.
13Color, Quantity, Quality, Odor.
14Wind Gate (mild) → Qi Gate (interior, stronger) → Life Gate (severe) → past the tip (life-threatening).
15Left index finger for boys, right index finger for girls.
16Left cheek=Liver, Forehead=Heart, Nose=Spleen, Right cheek=Lung, Chin=Kidney — all patient's own left/right; face-forward diagrams mirror this (patient's left appears on the viewer's right).
17Having Shen: bright/moist complexion, alert eyes, clear speech, quick response, even respiration. Loss of Shen: dim/dull, blurred vision, unconscious/delirious, sluggish, rapid/feeble respiration. False Shen: dull-then-sudden brightening across all categories.
18It's a critical-condition warning sign — a sudden brightening/clarity in an otherwise declining patient. Don't mistake it for genuine recovery.
19Papule=raised/red/seed-sized, fades w/ pressure. Macule=flat/red patch, doesn't fade w/ pressure. Blister=fluid-filled. Nodule=solid/palpable/round. (Any 4 with distinguishing features.)
20Parents' general health, health at time of conception, mother's health during pregnancy.
