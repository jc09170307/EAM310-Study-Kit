TrapCorrect Discriminator
Red throat = always excessChronic, dry, NOT swollen red throat can be Yin-deficiency empty heat instead

"Slow movement + cold" = Yin deficiencyIt's Yang deficiency. Yin deficiency causes HEAT signs, not cold
Any yellow finding = heatYellow = damp/deficiency in general; only bright/orange leans heat
